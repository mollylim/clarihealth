2. What data we collect and why

This website is informational only. We do not use signup forms, mailing lists, or tools that identify you as you browse.

Simply visiting our website does not require you to provide any personal data, and we do not collect your name, address, phone number, or any health or medical information through the site itself.

If you choose to email us at hello@clarihealth.uk, we will receive your email address and any information you include in your message so that we can respond to your enquiry.

3. Legal basis for processing

If you contact us by email, we process your information on the basis of our legitimate interest in responding to your enquiry and managing our business correspondence. You are under no obligation to contact us, and you can choose what information to include in your message.

4. How we store and process your data

Emails you send us are stored in our email system and handled only by people who need to see them in order to respond. We do not use third-party email marketing services on this website.

5. Cookies and analytics

We do not use analytics, advertising, or marketing cookies on this website.

Our website is hosted on Netlify, which may process basic technical information such as IP addresses and page requests for security and performance purposes. This information is handled by Netlify and is not shared with us in a form that identifies individual visitors.

6. How long we keep your data

We keep emails and related correspondence only for as long as needed to respond to your enquiry and for reasonable business record-keeping. You can ask us to delete correspondence at any time by contacting hello@clarihealth.uk.

7. Sharing your data

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. We may share information only where necessary to operate the website or respond to your enquiry, for example with our hosting or email providers acting as data processors.

8. Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the following rights:

  • Right to access — you can request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Right to rectification — you can ask us to correct any inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure — you can ask us to delete your personal data
  • Right to object — you can object to our processing of your personal data in certain circumstances
  • Right to complain — you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk if you believe we have not handled your data correctly

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at hello@clarihealth.uk.

9. Children's data

Our website is not directed at children under the age of 16 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with their personal data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time as our platform develops. We will always update the "last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to check back periodically.
